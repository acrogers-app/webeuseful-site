RLFGC Grace — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

RLFGC Grace ("Grace") is an internal sermon publishing tool operated by Webeuseful (Allen Rogers, allen@webeuseful.com) exclusively for Rehoboth Lighthouse Full Gospel Church Inc. ("the church"). Grace connects to the church's own YouTube account with the church's authorization. It has no public sign-up and no users other than the church and its authorized staff.

Google user data Grace accesses

With the church's OAuth consent, Grace uses the YouTube Data API with these scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload sermon-only videos and short highlight clips to the church's own channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl — to read and manage the church channel's own videos: listing uploads, reading and updating video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails), downloading captions of the church's own recordings, and scheduling publication.

How Grace uses this data

  • Reads the church channel's own video list, metadata, and captions to locate the sermon portion of each service recording.
  • Prepares edited sermon-only videos and short clips from those recordings.
  • Uploads the prepared videos to the same church channel, and updates their metadata — only after a member of church staff explicitly reviews and approves each item.

Grace never accesses any Google account data other than the church's own YouTube channel content, and never acts on any channel other than the church's.

Storage and retention

  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest on church-authorized infrastructure and are never shared with third parties.
  • Video files and transcripts being processed are stored temporarily on the operator's processing machine and deleted after publication.
  • Processing metadata (video IDs, titles, timestamps, approval records) is stored in a private database accessible only to the operator and church staff.

Sharing

Grace does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with any third party. Data is not used for advertising. The only "sharing" that occurs is the intended one: videos the church approves are published to the church's own public YouTube channel.

Limited Use disclosure

Grace's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The church can revoke Grace's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation immediately prevents any further API access. Stored tokens are then deleted on request.

Contact
