RLFGC Grace

Sermon publishing assistant for Rehoboth Lighthouse Full Gospel Church Inc. (Haverhill, MA — rlfgc.com)

RLFGC Grace is an internal tool operated for the church by Webeuseful (Allen Rogers). It helps church staff publish weekly service recordings in more accessible formats:

  • Prepares sermon-only videos from full Sunday service and Bible study recordings on the church's own YouTube channel.
  • Prepares short highlight clips of key moments from each message.
  • After explicit review and approval by church staff, uploads the prepared videos back to the church's own YouTube channel.

Grace works only with the church's own content and only on behalf of the church's own YouTube account. It is not a public service, does not have public sign-up, and is not available to any other users or channels.
