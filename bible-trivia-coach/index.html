Bible Study Coach

Gentle daily Scripture reading and low‑pressure quizzes, guided by a coach who's always glad you showed up. No guilt, no streak anxiety — just a few kind minutes with God's Word each day.

Open Web App Privacy Policy

Each day, a little more light

  • A short Gospel reading from a 30‑day plan.
  • A friendly 5‑question daily challenge on what you just read.
  • A verse of the day with a quick reflection quiz.

Learn the whole story

  • Quiz yourself on any book of the Bible — or a single chapter.
  • Bible history questions for the bigger picture.
  • Easy, Medium, Hard, or Mixed levels, so beginners and lifelong readers both feel at home.
  • Missed questions are saved so you can practice them again — no shame, just another look.

Listen as you read

  • Read‑along audio with word‑by‑word highlighting.
  • Choose your voice and reading speed.

Family Night

  • Take turns with up to 6 players and a shared score.
  • Questions the whole table can enjoy — from young readers to grandparents.

Private by default

  • No accounts, no sign‑in, no ads.
  • Your name, streaks, and scores never leave your device.
  • Anonymous quiz statistics help improve questions — and you can turn them off in Settings.
  • Full details in the privacy policy.
